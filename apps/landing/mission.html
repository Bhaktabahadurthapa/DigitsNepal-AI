01 / MISSION

Make AI practical, responsible, and reachable.

We exist to help people across Nepal understand AI, use it with judgment, and build solutions that serve their communities.

WHY WE EXIST

Technology becomes opportunity only when people can use it.

AI is changing education, work, public service, and entrepreneurship. Yet access to useful learning remains uneven—especially when material is expensive, highly technical, or unavailable in Nepali.

DigitsNepal AI closes that gap through free bilingual learning, educator-supervised tools, and open projects rooted in real needs.

AI drafts. People decide. Evidence comes before confidence.

HOW WE WORK

01

Access first

Core learning is free for individuals and designed for different devices and connectivity levels.

02

Bilingual by design

English and Nepali are part of the product—not an afterthought.

03

Human responsibility

Educators review courses and people approve consequential outputs.

04

Verifiable trust

Tutors cite approved sources, admit uncertainty, and operate within safety and cost limits.
