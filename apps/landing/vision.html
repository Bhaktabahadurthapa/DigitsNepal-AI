02 / VISION

A Nepal that builds with AI—not only consumes it.

By 2030, we want practical AI literacy and responsible agent-building skills to be within reach of every motivated learner and educator.

THE FUTURE WE SEEK

Local capability. Shared knowledge. Measurable public value.

We imagine learners moving from curiosity to competence, educators creating trusted local material, and organizations using supervised AI to improve real services.

LearnUnderstand AI in a language and context that make sense.
BuildCreate useful, evaluated, human-supervised agents.
ServeApply capability to education and community challenges.

VISION PILLARS

01

Every learner can begin

Clear foundations, mobile-friendly lessons, and bilingual explanations reduce the cost of entry.

02

Educators remain authors

AI speeds up drafting, but educators own review, context, and publication.

03

Agents earn trust

Useful agents cite sources, respect boundaries, and can be evaluated before use.

04

Progress is evidence-led

We publish outcomes only after we can verify how they were counted.
